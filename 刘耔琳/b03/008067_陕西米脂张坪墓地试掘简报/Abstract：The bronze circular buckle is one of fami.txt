# 陕西米脂张坪墓地试掘简报
# 章节: Abstract：The bronze circular buckle is one of familiar decorations on clothing in north China during

Eastern Zhou Dynasty. Based on the typological and chronological analysis, this article gives a
discussion for their distribution on time and space. Archaeological evidence showed, the earliest
bronze circular buckle was found in Middle of Great-Wall area, then they diffused to northern of Hebei
area and Gan –Ning area.we probes into the evolvement and origin of bronze circular buckle. Comparing
the information with China and foreign area，we find it is possible that the bronze circular buckle origins
from the buckle of horse in central plain region and middle Asia area.
Key words: Bronze circular buckle; northern zone of China; Eastern Zhou Dynasty.
