閣羅鳳
所規置也。嘗為信州地。城池郭邑皆如漢制。州中南北二十餘里，東西四十五里。帶邑及過山雖有三千餘戶，田疇多廢，閭里少人。

地在池東南隅。故渭北節度段子英，此州人也。故居、墳墓皆在。雲南東第二程有欠舍川，大都部落第三程至石鼓驛，舊化川也。第四程至曲驛，有大覽賧、小覽賧，漢舊覽州也。
Yunnan Cheng was planned and established by Ge Luofeng during the Tianbao period. 
It was once part of Xinzhou territory. 
The city walls, outer defenses, and towns were all modeled after Han dynasty systems. 
From north to south within the state was more than twenty li, and from east to west forty-five li. 
Although there were over three thousand households in the surrounding towns and across the mountains, much farmland had been abandoned, and the neighborhoods were sparsely populated. 
Zhuge Liang established Yunnan Jun to the northeast of Yongchang; this is precisely its former site. 
To the west, across the mountains, there was Pinbixi Qia, also known as Qingzi Chuan, which had once been Bo Zhou. 
A large pond encircled the mountain and stretched more than twenty li in length; the abandoned site of Bo Zhou was located at the southeast corner of the pond. 
Duan Ziying, former Weibei Jiedu, was a native of this state. 
His former residence and tomb are still there. 
Two li east of Yunnan, in the second stage, was Qianshe Chuan; three li from the Dadau tribe's settlement to Shigu Yizhi marked the third stage and was known as Jiuhua Chuan. 
The fourth stage led to Qu Yizhi, where there were Dalan Qia and Xiaolan Qia; these were the old Han dynasty Lan Zhou.

弄棟城在故姚州川中，南北百餘里，東西三十餘里。廢城在東巖山上。當川中有平巖，周迴五六頃，新築弄棟城在其上。管雜蠻數部落，悉無漢人。姚州百姓陷蠻者，皆被移隸遠處。
Nongdong Cheng was located in the central part of Gu Yaozhou Chuan, stretching more than one hundred li from north to south and over thirty li from east to west. 
The abandoned city lay on Dongyan Mountain. 
In the central part of the plain there was a flat rock, surrounding five or six qing in area; the newly built Nongdong Cheng stood on top of it. 
It administered several tribes of Zaza Miao, all of whom were without Han Chinese people. 
The common people of Yao Zhou who had fallen into the hands of the Miao tribes were all relocated to distant places.

Zhedong Cheng was established by Feng Jiayi in the second year of Guangde. 
The area had been the old Han dynasty Kun Chuan, hence it was called Kunchi. 
To the northeast were well-dwelling city walls of Jingyi Chenghuang, and to the west of the city was Han Cheng; local tradition claimed it was the old city of Zhuang Qiao. 
More than ten li east of the city lay Guchang Cun, which was said to be the former territory of the Han dynasty Guchang Wang. 
In the tenth year of Zhenyuan, Nanjiao defeated Xirong and relocated tens of thousands of households from Shi, Shun, and Moshe ethnic groups to settle in this area. 
Additionally, more than a thousand households from Yongchang, including Wangjuzi and Wangwaiyu, were relocated to the vicinity of the city to stabilize the roads.

晉寧州，漢滇河故地也。在柘東城南八十里晉平川，幅員數百里，西爨王墓，纍纍相望。
Jinning Zhou was the former territory of Han Dianhe. 
It lay in Jinping Chuan, eighty li south of Zhedong Cheng, covering hundreds of li. The tombs of the western Cuan kings were numerous and visible from one another.

Shicheng Chuan was the former site of Wei Xian. 
In the middle of Zhenguan, it became Lang Zhou; in the early years of Kaiyuan, it was renamed Nanning Zhou. 
The city walls were those of the old city where Zhuge Liang had once fought a battle. 
Inside the city were writings composed by Zhuge Liang, inscribed on two stone tablets. The engraved text on their backs read: "If this tablet falls over, the Miao will become Han slaves." 
In recent years, the Miao and Yi people have used wooden supports to prop up the columns.

石城南面有新豐川，漢南寧州新豐縣故地也。廢城墻塹猶在，大小石城川同。
To the south of Shicheng lay Xinfeng Chuan, which was the former site of Xinfeng Xian in Han Nanjing Zhou. 
The walls and trenches of the abandoned city still remained; this applied equally to Daxiaoshi Cheng Chuan.

To the southwest of Shengma Chuan lay Qu'e Chuan, which was Tongqi Xian in Han Naning Zhou.

Anning Zhen was one day's journey west of Zhedong Cheng and the former site of Lianran Xian. 
Tonghai Zhen, three stages to the west of Anning, reached Longfeng Yizhi. 
In front of the yizhi lay a marshy plain; it was eight days' journey from Zhedong Cheng and was the former site of Han Yuyuan Xian.

Fourteen days' journey south of Tonghai Cheng reached Butou. From Butou, one could travel by boat along the river for thirty-five days to leave Nanman territory. The local Yi people did not understand boats and ships; many instead chose to take a bold overland route from Tonghai Cheng into Zhen Zhou, Deng Zhou, Linxi Yuan, and traveled via Feng Zhou's road. 
Liangshui Chuan extended southwest to Longhe, then further south in a straight line along the Qingmuxiang Mountain Road, reaching Kunlun Guo. 
9 
寧北城，在漢桑榆縣之東境也。本無城池，今以浪人詔矣羅君舊宅為理所。東地有野共川，北地有虺川，又北有樻川，又北有郎婆川，又北有桑川，即至鐵橋城北九賧川。又西北有羅眉川，又西牟郎共城，又西至傍彌潛城。西有鹽井，鹽井西有斂尋城。皆施蠻、順蠻部落今所
居之地也。又西北至聿齎城，又西北至弄視川。
Ningbei Cheng was located on the eastern border of Han Sangyu Xian. 
Originally there were no city walls; now, the residence of Langer Zhaoyi Luojun was used as an administrative office. 
To the east was Yegong Chuan; to the north, Hui Chuan. Further north lay Fang Chuan, then Langpo Chuan, and further still, Sang Chuan, reaching Jiugai Chuan to the north of Tieqiao Cheng. 
To the northwest was Luomei Chuan; further west lay Moulanggong Cheng, and still farther to the west reached Bangmi Qian Cheng. 
To the west were salt wells, and beyond them to the west lay Lianxun Cheng. 
These were all lands currently inhabited by tribes of Shi Miao and Shun Miao. 
Further northwest was Yujici Cheng, then still further northwest lay Nongshi Chuan.

鐵橋城在劍川北三日程，川中平路有驛。
貞元
十年
，南詔蒙
異牟尋
用軍破東西兩城，斬斷鐵橋，大籠官已下投水死者以萬計。今西城南詔置兵守御，東城至神川以來，半為散地。見管浪加萌、於浪、傳兗、長褌、磨些、撲子、河人、弄棟等十餘種。
Tieqiao Cheng was three days' journey north of Jianchuan; in the central plain there were roads with yizhi stations. 
In the tenth year of Zhenyu, Nanjiao Meng Yimouxun led his army to capture both eastern and western cities, cut down Tieqiao Bridge, and over ten thousand people from Dalongguan downwards drowned in the water. 
Now, Nanjiao has stationed troops to defend the western city; from the eastern city to Shenchuan onward, half of it is now scattered and uncontrolled territory. 
It currently administers more than ten ethnic groups, including Langjia Meng, Yulang, Chuan Yuan, Changkuan, Moshe, Puzi, He Ren, and Nongdong. 
11 
昆明城，在東瀘之西，去龍口十六日程。正北有諱苴川，正南至松外城，又正南至龍怯河，西南至小婆城，又西
南至大婆城，西北至三探覽城，又西北至鐵橋東城。其鐵橋上下乃昆明、雙舍，至松外已東，邊近瀘水，並磨些種落所居之地。
Kunming Cheng was located to the west of Donglu, sixteen days' journey from Longkou. 
Directly to the north was Huijucha; directly south reached Songwai Cheng, and further directly south lay Longqie He. To the southwest was Xiaopo Cheng, then farther southwest was Dapo Cheng. To the northwest was San Tanlan Cheng, and still further northwest was Tieqiao Dongcheng. 
The area above and below the iron bridge, namely Kunming and Shuangshe, to the east of Songwai, near Lushui River, was all inhabited by Moshe tribes. 
12

閣羅鳳

Yongchang Cheng, an ancient Ailao territory, lay six days' journey west of Diancang Shan. 
To the northwest, Guangdang Cheng was sixty days' journey away. 
Guangdang Cheng bordered Tubo territory. 
Across the Hou Xueshan, on the western side of Daidong Chuan, there was also a city built by Zhuge Wuhou. 
Inside the city was a temple of gods, which local people universally revered and worshipped without fail. 
The Miao tribesmen on horseback would dismount and run toward the temple from a distance, showing reverence. 
To the southwest was Zhanan Cheng; local tradition referred to it as Yaozhen. 
Directly south, crossing Tangfeng Chuan led to Mangtian Lian. 
To the west of Lancang Jiang, across Puzi Qia, all these tribes were Wangjuzi. 
The people valued bravery and strength, and the land also had many horses. 
Before Kaiyuan it was cut off from contact, with no communication between it and the Six Zhao. 
Sheng Luopi first abandoned Zheyu Cheng; after Ge Luofeng, the region gradually became pacified and submitted. 
The total number of Nanjiao troops was about thirty thousand, with Yongxi accounting for one part of it. 
Additionally, there were mixed tribes such as Jinchi, Qichi, Yinchi, Xiu Jiao, Chuansi, Luoxing, Moshe, and Wangwaiyu. All of them required three or four interpreters to communicate, yet their language was still connected with that of Heqia. 
13

Yinsheng Cheng was located south of Puzia, ten days' journey from Longwei Cheng. To its southeast lay Tongdeng Chuan; directly to the south was Hepu Chuan, and straight further south was Qianglang Chuan, all of which were border regions near the sea with no human habitation. 
To the east was Songjiang Chuan; to the south, Qionge Chuan. Further south lay Linji Chuan, and southeast of that was Dayingkong. Still further south were several tribes including Parame, Bose, Shepo, Boni, and Kunlun. 
The region had many places for external trade, with abundant treasures; gold and musk were the most valued goods. 
There were also dozens of tribes such as Puzi and Changzong Miao. 
Kainan Cheng was eleven days' journey south of Longwei Cheng, administering Liu Zhuihe Dudu Cheng. It also included Weiyuan Cheng, Fengyi Cheng, and Lilun Cheng, with over one hundred salt wells within its territory. 
Ten tribes including Mangnai Dao and Heichi were all subject to it. 
By land, it was ten days' journey from Yongchang; by water, thirty days' journey downstream to Michen Guo. 
To the south lay the South Sea, three days' journey from Kunlun. 
In between were also five tribes of ethnic groups such as Mujialuo, Qian Ni, and Liqiangzi under its jurisdiction. 
Yueli Cheng was located north of Yongchang, administering Changbang and Tengwan. 
Changbang Cheng had high mountains on three sides and faced Lu 
Yuedou
 River. 
To the south of Tengwan Cheng lay Moshile Cheng; to its southwest was Luojunxun Cheng. 
Further west led to Ke Cheng, crossing Shuilangyang Chuan; going directly south over the mountains reached Yaxi Cheng. 
Further south was Shouwai Chuan; then westward to Mang Buluo, further still west to Yanjing, and finally west to Bao Ao He. 
Lishui Cheng and Xunchuan Dachuan Cheng were located east of the river. 
From Shanglang Ping, going north to Limierjia Yanjing and then to Anxi Cheng, a straight path north led to Xiaopomen Guo. 
To the east was Baoshan Cheng; further west across Lishui River lay Jinbao Cheng. 
To the southwest of Meiluojia was Jinsheng Cheng. 
From Jinbao Cheng, going north to Moulang Cheng across Lishui River led back to Jinbao Cheng. 
From Jinbao Cheng, heading west reached Daoji Chuan; northeastward to Menbo Cheng, and northwestward to Guangdang Cheng, bordering Tubo territory. 
Facing north toward the snow mountains, the tribes under its jurisdiction were the same as those of Zhenxi Cheng. 
Zhenxi Cheng's southern boundary reached Cangwang Cheng, bordering Lishui River; to its northeast was Micheng, and to its northwest lay the Lishui ferry. 
South of the Lishui Ferry was Qixian Shan; on the mountain's western side stood Shenlong He Zha. 
To the west of Qixian was where the Luoxing Miao lived. 
The administrative center of Moling Dudu Cheng was located on a mountain. From Xunchuan and Qixian onward, the area was filled with malarial toxins; the land was flat as a grindstone, where in winter trees and grass never withered, and the sun set from among the grasses. 
The officials of various cities and towns feared malarial diseases; some even stayed away in other places, avoiding direct involvement in their duties. 
Nanjiao specially built a city on Moling Mountain, placing trusted people in charge to administer the affairs of five regions: Xunchuan, Changbang, Moling, Jin, and Micheng. 
It administered more than ten tribes, including Jinchi, Qichi, Xiu Jiao, Xiumian, Diaoti, and Sengqi.
