 陕西西安白杨寨晚唐壁画墓M1373发掘简报


            陕西省考古研究院



  关键词：唐代，壁画墓，宦官，刘行深，家族墓

  摘要：2022年，陕西省考古研究院于西安市浐灞生态区白杨寨村南发掘了一座晚唐时期砖室壁画墓。该墓破

坏严重，但其壁画内容丰富，色彩艳丽，时代特征明显。该墓所处位置为刘弘规家族墓地，根据墓葬形制及位置关

系，推测墓主应为刘弘规家族第二代成员，即晚唐时期权宦刘行深或其夫人。该墓是目前考古发现的唯一一座刘弘

规子辈成员墓葬，为研究晚唐时期家族墓地布局提供了重要资料，而出土的精美壁画，为近年来所罕见，对研究唐

宋之际壁画题材及风格转变有着重要意义。
   KEYWORDS: Tang Dynasty, Mural tomb, Liu Xingshen, Eunuch, Family cemetery
   ABSTRACT: In 2022, the Shaanxi Academy of Archaeology excavated a mural tomb of the Late Tang Dynasty located
to the south of Baiyangzhai Village in the Chan-ba Ecological Zone, Xi’an. While the tomb has been severely damaged, its
murals are well preserved, providing rich, colorful and distinctive contents for archaeological study. According to previously
known information, the tomb is located in the cemetery of the Liu Honggui’s family. Based on the shape and location of the
tomb, it can be surmised that the owner of the tomb should be a second-generation member of Liu Honggui’s family, probably
Liu Xingshen or his wife; the former was a powerful eunuch in the Late Tang Dynasty. This burial is the only tomb of Liu
Honggui’s descendants found from archaeological excavations, which provides important information for the study of the
layout of family cemeteries in the Late Tang Dynasty. The exquisite murals unearthed are rarely seen among Tang-Dynasty
tombs that have been found in recent years, and are of great signiﬁcance for the study of the thematic and stylistic changes of
murals during the Tang and Song Dynasties.
