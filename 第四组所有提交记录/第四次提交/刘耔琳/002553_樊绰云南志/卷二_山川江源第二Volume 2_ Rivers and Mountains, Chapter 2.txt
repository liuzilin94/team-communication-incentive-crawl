金馬山在柘東城螺山南二十餘里，高百餘丈，與碧雞山東南西北相對。土俗傳云，昔有金馬，往往出見，山上亦有神祠。從漢界入蠻路，出此山之下。螺山偏地悉是螺蛤，故以名焉。
Jinma Shan is located twenty li south of Luoshan in Zhedong Cheng, over one hundred zhang high, facing the southeast and northwest directions with Bijiji Shan. 
The local tradition says that in ancient times there was a golden horse which often appeared; there is also a shrine to the gods on the mountain. 
The road from the Han territory into the southern frontier passes beneath this mountain. 
In the surrounding areas of Luoshan, there are many snails and clams; hence it was named after that. 
2 
碧雞山在昆池西岸上，與柘東城隔水相對。從東來者
岡頭數十里已見此山。山勢特秀，池水清澹，水中有碧雞山石，山有洞庭樹，年月久遠，空有餘本。
Biji Ji Shan is located on the western bank of Kunchi, facing Zhedong Cheng across the water. 
Those coming from the east can see this mountain after dozens of li at the head of the gao. 
The mountain is especially beautiful, with clear and tranquil water in the pool. In the water there are stones of Bijiji Shan; on the mountain there are Dongting trees, which have been there for a long time, now only remnants remain. 
3

Diancang Shan 
Note: Dian in the "Tang Shu" is written as Dian
, extends from Shi Qiao in the south to Dengchuan in the north, over 150 li long, and is named Diancang. 
It runs straight from north to south, but is not very regular in alignment. 
Facing east toward Erhe River, the city walls and settlements are arranged like a chessboard at the foot of the mountain. 
The western side is steep and precipitous, overlooking an open plain below. 
The mountain peak rises several thousand zhang high; the stone ridges are dark green, with no paths accessible to people. 
Snow occasionally falls there in winter. 
4 
囊蔥山在西洱河東隅，河流俯齧山根。土山無樹石，高處不過數十丈。面對賓居、越析，山下有路，從渠斂趙出
登川。
镂 Nangcong Shan is located at the eastern corner of Erhe River, with the river flowing downward and eroding its base. 
The earthen mountain has no trees or rocks, and its height does not exceed dozens of zhang. 
Facing Binju and Yuexi, there is a road at the foot of this mountain leading from Qu Lian Zhao to Dengchuan. 
5 
高黎共山在永昌西，下臨怒江。左右平川，謂之穹賧，湯浪加萌所居也。草木不枯，有瘴氣。自永昌之越賧，途經此山，一驛在山之半，一驛在山之巔。朝濟怒江登山，暮方到山頂。冬中山上積雪苦寒，夏秋又苦穹賧、湯浪毒暑酷熱。河賧賈客在尋傳羈離未還者為之謠曰：「冬時欲歸來，高黎共上雪。秋夏欲歸來，無梆穹賧熱。春時欲歸來，平中絡賂絕。」
絡賂，財之名也
Gaoli Gong Shan is located west of Yongchang, overlooking Nujiang River below. 
The flat plains on both sides are called Qiongdi, where Tanglang Jiameng reside. 
Grasses and trees do not wither here; there is malarial vapor. 
Traveling from Yongchang to Yuedi, the route passes through this mountain; one post station is located halfway up the mountain, and another is at its summit. 
Starting in the morning by crossing Nujiang River to ascend the mountain, one only reaches the summit by evening. 
In winter, snow accumulates on the mountain and it is bitterly cold; in summer and autumn, one suffers from the intense heat of Qiongdi and Tanglang. 
The merchants from Hedui, who were detained in Xunchuan and had not yet returned, composed a song saying: "In winter I wish to return home, but there is snow on Gaoli Gong." 
"In summer and autumn I also wish to return, yet Qiongdi is unbearably hot." 
"In spring I want to come back, but the road through Pingzhong is cut off by bandits." 
Luo lu refers to money or wealth.

6 
大雪山在永呂西北，從騰充過寶山城，又過金寶城以北大賧，周迥百餘里，悉皆野蠻，無君長也。地有瘴毒，河賧人至彼中瘴者，十有八九死。閣羅鳳嘗使領軍將於大賧中築城，管制野蠻。不逾周歲，死者過半，遂罷棄，不復往來。其山上肥沃，種瓜瓠長丈餘，冬瓜亦然，皆三尺圍。又多薏苡，無農桑，收此充糧。三面皆占大雪山，其高處造天。往往有吐蕃至賧貨易，云此山有路，去贊普牙帳不遠。
Daxueshan is located northwest of Yonglv. From Tengchong, passing through Baoshan Cheng and then Jinbao Cheng to the north, one enters Badui. The surrounding area spans over a hundred li, all inhabited by wild southern frontier tribes without chieftains or rulers. 
The land is afflicted with malarial toxins; people from Hedui who fall ill to the miasma there, eight or nine out of ten die. 
Ge Luofeng once sent a military commander to build a city in Badui, to control the wild southern frontier tribes. 
Within less than one year, more than half of them died; thus it was abandoned and no longer visited. 
The soil on the mountain is fertile; gourds planted there can grow over one zhang long, and winter melons are also like this, with a circumference of three chi. 
There is also an abundance of yiyi; there are no farming or sericulture activities here, and the people rely on this crop to supply their food. 
Three sides are occupied by Daxueshan, whose highest peaks reach up to the sky. 
Tubo people often come here for trade and say that there is a road on this mountain, not far from the Zanpu's yurt. 
7 
又有水，源出臺登山，南流過巂州，西南至會州諾賧與東瀘，古諾水也。源出蕃中節度北，謂之諾矣江，南郎部落，又東折流至尋傳部落，與磨些江合。源出吐蕃中節度西共籠川犛牛石下，故謂之犛牛河。環遶弄視川，南流過鐵橋上下磨些部落，即謂之磨些江。至尋傳與東瀘水合，東北過會同川，總名瀘水。蜀忠武侯諸葛亮伐南蠻，五月渡瀘水處，在弄棟城北，今謂之南瀘。兩岸葭，大如臂脛。川中氣候常熱，雖至冬，行過者皆袒衣流汗。
又東北入戎州界為馬湖，至關邊縣門，與朱提江合，流戎門南城入外江。
There is also a river, originating from Taideng Shan, flowing south past Xizhou, and then southwest to Huizhou Nuodui and Donglu. This was the ancient Nu Shui River. 
Its source is north of the Jiedu in Tubo, called Nuoyi Jiang. It flows south through Nanlang Buluo and then turns eastward to Xunchuan Buluo, where it joins Moxie Jiang. 
Its source is at the western part of Gonglong Chuan, under the Lainiu Shi in the Jiedu area of Tubo; thus it is called Lainiu He. 
It circles around Nongshi Chuan, flowing south through Shangxia Moxie Buluo at Tieqiao, and is called Moxie Jiang there. 
It flows to Xunchuan where it merges with Donglu Shui, then northeast through Huitong Chuan; collectively they are known as Lu Shui. 
When Zhuge Liang, the Loyal Martial Lord of Shu, campaigned against the southern frontier tribes, he crossed Lu Shui in May. The crossing was north of Nongdong Cheng; it is now called Nanlu. 
The reeds on both banks are as thick as arms and legs. 
The climate in the plain is always hot; even in winter, travelers passing through sweat profusely with bare skin. 
Flowing northeast into Rongzhou territory, it becomes Mahu Lake. It reaches Guanbian Xianmen and merges with Zhuti Jiang, flowing through Nan Cheng at Rongmen to enter Waijiang River.

Dianchi Shui is also known as Dongkunchi; its waters flow southwest around the mountain, then northwest into a river that passes beneath Anning Cheng. 
The water stretches east to west with thirty bridges; one of them is over three hundred bu wide and long. 
It takes seven days on foot to reach where it converges with Lu Shui. 
Youliangshui Chuan is located two days' journey south of Dianchi; it was the old Han state of Lizhou. 
There is a large pool in the middle of the plain, and its water flows eastward. 
The water flows out from within a stone cave; the stream is very wide, but the cave entrance is quite narrow. 
The local people say that if the cave suddenly becomes empty, the common folk will worry about drowning. 
Xinfeng Chuan also has a large and wide pool.

瀾滄江，源出吐蕃中大雪山下莎川。東南過聿齎城西，謂之瀨水河，又過順蠻部落。南流過劍川大山之西。瀾滄江南流入海。龍尾城西第七驛有橋，即永昌也。兩岸高險，水迅激。橫亙大竹索為梁，上布簀，簀上實板，仍通
以竹屋蓋橋。其穿索石孔，孔明所鑿也。昔諸葛徵永昌，於此築城。今江西山上有廢城遺跡及古碑猶存，亦有神祠廟存焉。
Lancang Jiang originates from Shachuan at the foot of Daxueshan in central Tubo. 
It flows southeast past Yujiji Cheng to its west, where it is called Laishui He; then it passes through Shunman Buluo. 
Flowing south, it passes the western side of Dashaan in Jianchuan. 
Lancang Jiang flows into the sea to the south. 
The seventh post station west of Longwei Cheng has a bridge, which is Yongchang. 
Both banks are high and perilous, with the water rushing violently. 
A large bamboo cable is stretched horizontally as a beam; mats are laid on top, wooden planks placed over the mats, and small bamboo huts cover the bridge. 
The stone holes through which the cables pass were drilled by Zhuge Liang. 
In ancient times, Zhuge Liang campaigned in Yongchang and built a city here. 
Now on the mountains to the west of Jiangxi, there are still ruins of an abandoned city and ancient stone tablets remaining; there is also a temple for gods that remains.

又彌諾江在麗水西，源出西北小婆羅門國。南流過湧腋苴川，又東南至兜彌伽木柵，分流繞柵，居沙灘南北一百里，東西六十里。合流正東，過彌臣國，南入於海。
Another Miluo Jiang is located west of Lishui, with its source in the northwest from Xiaopomen Guo. 
It flows south through Yongye Juchuan, then southeast to Doumi Qiumu Zha. The river divides and circles around the Zha, with sandy beaches stretching one hundred li north and south, and sixty li east and west. 
The rivers converge to the exact east, passing through Michen Guo, then flowing into the sea in the south.
