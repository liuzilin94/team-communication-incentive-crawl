𧸘者，州之名號也。韋齊休《雲南行記》有十𧸘，字作此𧸘字。案：「𧸘」字，原本誤作「臉」，今從《一統志》改正。又韋齊休《雲南行記》七字原本誤作大書，今亦改正。
The term "Xia" refers to a name of a prefecture. 
Wei Qixiu's Yunan Xingji mentions ten Xia, using this character "Xia" for the term. 
Note: The character "Xia," originally mistakenly written as "lian," is now corrected according to the Yitongzhi. 
Moreover, in Wei Qixiu's Yunan Xinge Ji, seven characters were originally mistakenly written as large script; they have now also been corrected. 
2 
大和、陽苴咩謂之陽𧸘，大釐謂之史𧸘，邆川謂之賧𧸘，蒙舍謂之蒙舍𧸘，白崖謂之勃弄𧸘
案：標題稱六𧸘，第十卷亦有六𧸘之文，此所別乃止五𧸘，疑後龍口一城當亦為一𧸘，係傳寫誤脫一句也
。雲南、柘東、永昌、寧北、鎮西及開南、銀生等七城則有大軍將領之，亦稱節
度。貞元十年，掠吐蕃，鐵橋城，今稱鐵橋節度，其餘鎮皆分隸焉
案：自「雲南柘東」至「皆分隸焉」五十二字，與六嶮文不相屬，疑為第六篇「雲南城鎮」條下之文，錯簡於此
。
Dahe and Yangjumei are referred to as Yangxia, Dali is called Shixia, Lichuan is known as Yuxia, Mengshe is termed Mengshe Xia, Baiya is named Bonong Xia 
Note: The title mentions Six Xia, and the Tenth Volume also contains references to six Xia; however, only five Xia are listed here. It is suspected that Longkou Cheng should be considered another Xia, likely omitted due to an error in transmission
. 
Yunnan, Zhedong, Yongchang, Ningbei, Zhenxi, and Kai'nan, Yingsheng, seven cities in total, are governed by major military commanders, also known as Jiedu. 
In the tenth year of Zhenyuan, they raided Tubo and Tieqiao Cheng. Today it is known as Tieqiao Jiedu, while other towns are all separately subordinated to it 
Note: The fifty-two characters from "Yunnan Zhedong" to "jie fen li yan" have no relation to the Six Xia text; it is suspected that this passage belongs under the sixth section titled "Yunnan Chengzhen," mistakenly placed here
. 
3 
大和城、大釐城、陽苴咩城，本皆河蠻所居之地也。開元二十五年，蒙歸義逐河蠻，奪據大和城。後數月，又襲破苴咩盛羅皮，取大釐城，仍築龍口城為保障。閣羅鳳多由大和、大釐、邆川來往。蒙歸義男等初立大和城，以為不安，遂改創陽苴咩城。
Dahe Cheng, Dali Cheng, and Yangjumei Cheng were originally inhabited by the He man people. 
In the twenty-fifth year of Kaiyuan, Meng Guiyi drove out the He man and seized Dahe Cheng. 
A few months later, he again attacked and defeated Ju' mie Shengluopi, captured Dali Cheng, and still built Longkou Cheng as a safeguard. 
Geluofeng often traveled between Dahe, Dali, and Lingchuan. 
Initially, Meng Guiyi Nan established Dahe Cheng, but considering it insecure, he then changed to build Yangjumei Cheng. 
4 
大和城，北去陽苴咩城一十五里。巷陌皆壘石為之，高丈餘，連延數里不斷。城中有大牌，閣羅清平官王蠻利之文
案：《舊唐書》閣羅鳳得西瀘令鄭回，甚愛重之，更名「蠻利」，後為清平官。此云「王蠻利」者，疑即其人也
。論阻絕皇化之由，受制西戎之意。
Dahe Cheng is fifteen li north of Yangjumei Cheng. 
The alleys and streets are all built with stone walls, more than one zhang high, stretching for several li without interruption. 
Inside the city there is a large plaque, inscribed with text by Wang Manli, an official of Qingping under Gelaofeng 
Note: According to the Old Book of Tang, Gelaofeng obtained Zheng Hui, the magistrate of Xilu, and greatly valued him. He changed his name to "Manli," who later became a Qingping official. 
This mention of "Wang Manli" is likely referring to this person.

Discuss the reasons for obstructing and severing imperial influence, and the meaning of being controlled by western barbarians. 
5 
龍口城，閣羅鳳所築。榮抱玷蒼南麓數里，城門臨洱水下。河上橋長百餘步，過橋分三路：直南蒙舍路，向西永昌路，向東白崖城路。
Longkou Cheng was built by Geluo Feng. 
It is located several li south of the foot of Diancang Mountain, with its city gate facing Er River below. 
A bridge over the river is more than a hundred bu long; after crossing the bridge, it divides into three roads: directly south to Mengshe Lu, westward to Yongchang Lu, and eastward to Baiya Cheng Lu. 
6 
大釐城，南去陽苴洋城四十里，北去龍口城二十五里，
邑居人戶尤眾盛，羅皮多在此城。并陽苴咩并邆川，今並南詔往來所居也。家室共守，五處如一。東南十餘里有舍利水城，在洱河中流島上。四面臨水，夏月最清涼，南詔常於此城避暑。
Dali Cheng is forty li south of Yangjumei Cheng and twenty-five li north of Longkou Cheng. The population in this area is particularly numerous, and Luo Pi often resides in this city. 
Combined with Yangjumei and Lingchuan, they are now the places where Nanzhao people travel between and reside. 
Families live together in defense; these five locations are as one. 
More than ten li southeast is Sheli Shui Cheng, located on an island in the middle of Er River. 
It is surrounded by water on all sides; it is especially cool and refreshing during summer, so Nanzhao rulers often came here to escape the heat. 
7 
陽苴咩城，南詔大衙門，上重樓，左右又有階道，高二丈餘。甃以青石為磴。樓前方二三里，南北城門相對。太和來往通衢也。從樓下門行三百步至第二重門，門屋五間，兩行門樓相對，各有榜，并清平官、大軍將、六曹長宅
也。入第二重門行二百餘步，至第三重門。門列戟，上有重樓。入門是屏墻，又行一百餘步至大廳，階高丈餘。重屋製如蛛網，架空無柱。兩邊皆有門樓，下臨清池。大廳後小廳，小廳後即南詔宅也。客館在門樓外東南二里。僅前有亭，亭臨方池，周回七里，水深數丈，魚鱉悉有。
Yangjumei Cheng is the main administrative center of Nanzhao, with upper story buildings and staircases on both sides, over two zhang high. 
The steps are paved with blue stone as treads. 
Two or three li in front of the building, there are northern and southern city gates facing each other. 
It is a major thoroughfare for travel between Taihe. 
Walking three hundred bu from the gate under the building leads to the second gate. The gate house has five rooms, with two rows of gate towers facing each other; each has a plaque, and these are the residences of Qingping officials, major military commanders, and heads of the six departments. 
After entering the second gate and walking more than two hundred bu, one reaches the third gate. 
Spear racks are arranged at the gate, with upper story buildings above them. 
After entering the gate is a screen wall; walking more than one hundred bu further leads to the main hall, with steps over one zhang high. 
The upper buildings are constructed like a spider's web, suspended in mid-air without pillars. 
lington Both sides have gate towers, with clear pools below them. 
Behind the main hall is a smaller hall; behind the smaller hall lies the residence of Nanzhao ruler. 
The guest house is located two li southeast outside the gate tower. 
There is only a pavilion in front, with the pavilion overlooking a square pond; it circles for seven li, the water is several zhang deep, and contains all kinds of fish and turtles. 
8 
邆川城，舊邆川也，南去龍口城十五里。初望父部落居之，後浪穹詔豐咩襲而奪之。豐咩孫鐸望與南詔戰敗，退保劍川南，遂有城。城依山足，東距瀘水，北有泥沙。自
閣羅鳳及異牟尋皆填固增修，最為名邑。東北有史郎川，又東祿諾品川，又北俄坤。
Lingchuan Cheng, the old Lingchuan, is fifteen li south of Longkou Cheng. 
Initially inhabited by the Wàngfù tribe, it was later seized by Langqiong Zhao Fengmie through attack. 
Fengmie's grandson Duowang, after being defeated in battle against Nanzhao, retreated to the south of Jianchuan and thus established a city. 
The city is built at the foot of the mountain; it lies east of Lushui River, with mud and sand to its north. 
From Geluo Feng to Yimou Xun, they all reinforced and expanded the city; it became the most renowned county. 
To the northeast is Shilang Chuan; further east is Luenopin Chuan, and to the north is Ekun. 
9 
蒙舍川，羅盛已上之地。舊為蒙舍州，去龍口城一日程。當五詔俱存，而蒙舍北有蒙巂詔，即楊瓜州也，同在一川，地氣有瘴，肥沃宜禾稻。又有大池，周回數十里，多魚及凌芡之屬。川中水東南與勃弄川合流。南有籠磨些川。凡邆川河，蒙舍謂之川賧。然邑落人眾，蔬果水凌之味，則蒙舍為尤殷。
Mengshe Chuan is the land above Luosheng. 
It was formerly Mengshe Zhou, a one-day journey from Longkou Cheng. 
When the Five Zhao were all in existence, Mengshe had Mengxu Zhao to its north, which is Yangguazhou. They shared the same plain; the land was fertile and suitable for growing rice, though it was prone to malarial conditions. 
There is also a large pond, circling several dozen li, with many fish and aquatic plants such as water caltrops. 
The river in the plain flows southeast and merges with Bonong Chuan. 
To the south is Longmaxie Chuan. 
All rivers in Lingchuan, Mengshe calls them Chuandui. 
However, with a large population and abundant vegetables, fruits, water plants, and other flavors, Mengshe is particularly rich in these. 
10 
渠斂趙，本河東州也。西巖有石和城。烏蠻謂之「土山坡陀」者，謂此州城及大和城俱在陂陀山上故也。州中列樹夾道為交流，村邑連甍，溝塍彌望。大族有王、楊、李、趙四姓，皆白蠻也。云是沮蒲州人，遷涉至此，因以名州焉。東北至毛郎川，又東北至賓居湯，又北至越析川，磨些詔故地也。
Qulian Zhao was originally Hedong Zhou. 
To the west of the cliff is Shuhe Cheng. 
The Wu man refer to it as "Tushuipo Tuo," meaning that both this state city and Dahe Cheng are located on the Piduoshan hill. 
Inside the prefecture, rows of trees line the roads for shade; villages and towns are connected with continuous rooftops, ditches and fields stretching as far as the eye can see. 
The major clans include the surnames Wang, Yang, Li, and Zhao; they are all Bai man people. 
It is said that they were originally from Jupu Zhou, migrated here, and thus the prefecture was named after them. 
To the northeast is Maoliang Chuan; further northeast is Binju Tang, and to the north is Yuexi Chuan, which was the former territory of Moshe Zhao. 
11 
白崖城在勃弄川，天寶中附于忠、城、陽等五州之城也。依山為城，高十丈，四面皆引水環流，惟開南北兩門。南
隅是舊城，周回二里。東北隅新城，大歷七年閣羅鳳新築也。周回四里。城北門外有慈竹叢，大如人脛，高百尺餘。地內有閣羅鳳所造大廳，修廊曲廳，廳後院橙枳青翠，俯臨北墉。舊城內有池方三百餘步，池中有樓舍，雲貯甲仗。川東西二十餘里，南北百餘里。清平官已下，官給分田，悉在。南詔親屬亦住此城傍。其南二十里有蠻子城，閣羅鳳庶弟誠節母子舊居也。正南去開南城十
一日程。
Baiya Cheng lies in Bonong Chuan; it is a city that, during the Tianbao period, was attached to Zhong Zhou, Cheng Zhou, Yang Zhou and other four states. 
The city is built along the mountain, with a height of ten zhang; water flows around it on all sides, and only two gates are opened to the north and south. 
The southern corner is the old city, with a circumference of two li. 
In the northeast corner is the new city, newly built by Geluo Feng in the seventh year of Dali. 
It has a circumference of four li. 
Outside the northern gate of the city is a grove of cizhu bamboo, as thick as a person's shins and over one hundred chi tall. 
Inside the area is a large hall built by Geluo Feng, with long corridors and curved halls; behind the hall, in the courtyard, orange trees and tangerine trees are lush and green, overlooking the northern wall. 
Inside the old city is a square pond over three hundred bu in size; there are pavilions and houses on the pond, said to store armor and weapons. 
The plain extends more than twenty li from east to west, and over one hundred li from north to south. 
Below the Qingping officials, government-issued divided fields are all present for each official. 
Relatives of Nanzhao also reside near this city. 
Twenty li to the south is Manzi Cheng, which was once the residence of Geluo Feng's concubine brother Chengjie and his mother. 
Directly south from Kai'nan Cheng is eleven days journey away.
