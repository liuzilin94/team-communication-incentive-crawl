山西省考古研究院
朔州市文物保护与利用服务中心
朔城区文化和旅游局
摘要：后寨墓地位于山西省朔州市窑子头乡后寨村西约 2 公里的山前黄土台地上，地处北方
长城沿线的农牧交错地带。 2019 年至 2020 年，山西省考古研究院联合朔州当地文物部门对该墓
地进行了考古发掘，共发现东周至辽金时期墓葬 400 余座，其中秦汉墓葬共 73 座，墓葬时代主
要集中在西汉早期和西汉晚期，墓葬形制及随葬器物与上世纪 80 年代平朔考古队在七里河北岸
发掘的同时期墓葬文化面貌基本一致，应与平朔秦汉墓群同为秦汉马邑城附属的墓葬区，为丰富
朔州地区秦汉时期文化面貌提供了新的材料。
关键词：朔州 后寨 秦汉 马邑墓群
Abstract: This cemetery is located on the loess platform in front of a mountain, about 2 kilometers
west of Houzhai Village, Yaozitou Township, Shuozhou City, Shanxi Province, Where is the transitional
area of agricultural area and animal husbandry along the Great Wall in north China. Since 2019, the Shanxi
Provincial Institute of Archaeology have excavated it with the local institutes till 2020. There are total 400
tombs discoveried from Eastern Zhou to Liao, Jin period. Among them, ther are 73 tobms dating back to Qin
and Han dynasties, mainly the Early Han and Late Han. The structures of these tombs and burial objects
are basically accordance with those tombs excavated on the north bank of Qilihe River in 1980s. Therefore,
these two cemeteries mentioned above are burial area affiliated with Mayi town during Qin and Han period,
which provide new materials for studying the cultural features of the Qin and Han period in Shuozhou area.
Keywords: Shuozhou Houzhai Qin and Han Tombs group in Mayi

二、年代推断
后寨秦汉墓葬中均未发现相关纪年材料，墓
葬间无叠压打破关系。结合墓葬形制及出土陶器，
通过与周边地区已发表的同时期墓葬材料进行比
对，初步推断其时代主要集中于西汉早期和西汉
晚期，尤以西汉晚期墓葬居多。
