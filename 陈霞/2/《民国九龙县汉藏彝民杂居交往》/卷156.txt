Abstract:
Jiulong
County
is
located
at
the
in-
tersection
of
Sichuan,
Yunna,
and
Xizang.
Over
thousands
of
years,
various
ethnic
groups
have
lived
and
developed
together
there.
While
preser-
ving
their
own
cultural
heritages,
they
have
ab-
sorbed
and
acculturated
the
characteristics
of
other
ethnic
groups,
thus
giving
rise
to
a
diversified
re-
gional
culture.
Originally
home
to
Tibetans,
Jiu-
long
County
saw
the
gradual
influx
of
Han,
Yi,
and
other
ethnic
groups
starting
from
the
Yuan
Dy-
nasty.
Through
various
historical
periods,
different
ethnic
groups
have
interwoven
within
a
shared
space.
The
multi-dimensional
relationships
estab-
lished
in
this
space
have
laid
the
groundwork
for
the
emergence
and
growth
of
the
inter-embedded
development
of
diverse
ethnic
cultures
in
Jiulong
County.
This
has
resulted
in
the
current
pattern
of
mixed
settlements
and
a
relationship
of
mutual
de-
pendence
and
mutual
benefit.
The
abundant
natural
resources
of
Jiulong
County
has
been
attracting
a
diverse
influx
of
mi-
grants,
and
the
utilization
of
these
natural
re-
sources
has
demonstrated
a
notable
synergistic
effect
on
fostering
inter-ethnic
embedding
and
co-
existence.
As
such,
complementary
resource
allo-
cation
plays
a
pivotal
role
in
promoting
ethnic
in-
ter-embedding
and
symbiosis.
Moreover,
economic
cooperation
not
only
deepens
mutual
understanding
and
trust
among
ethnic
peoples
but
also
promotes
the
adaptation
and
transformation
of
livelihood
modes.
The
shared
inheritance
of
cultural
re-
sources
fosters
the
prosperous
development
of
the
social
economy.
Ethnic
policies
have
also
played
a
significant
guiding
role
in
economic
symbiosis,
en-
suring
an
equitable
distribution
and
sustainable
uti-
lization
of
resources,
thereby
reinforcing
the
inter-
embedded
and
symbiotic
relationships
among
eth-
nic
groups.
Generally
speaking,
the
interplay
of
ethnic
policies,
natural
resource
utilization,
social
networks,
and
other
factors,
has
collectively
con-
tributed
to
multi-ethnic
inter-embedding
and
coex-
istence
in
Jiulong
County.
The
transformation
of
livelihood
patterns
has
been
an
important
driving
force
in
the
evolution
of
ethnic
relations.
Jiulong
County's
shift
from
tradi-
tional
agriculture
and
nomadic
economy
to
a
mod-
ern
diversified
industrial
structure
has
not
only
changed
local
production
and
lifestyle
but
also
re-
shaped
the
landscape
of
ethnic
interactions,
influ-
encing
the
development
of
ethnic
relations.
As
eco-
nomic
exchanges
and
cooperation
intensify,
the
in-
terests
and
destinies
of
ethnic
groups
have
become
increasingly
intertwined,
triggering
endogenous
momentum
for
cultural
mutual
learning
and
shifting
cultural
identities.
Sustainable
harmony
in
ethnic
relations
relies
on
the
dual
construction
and
dy-
namic
equilibrium
of
material
foundations
and
spir-
itual
bonds.
In
summary,
the
symbiotic
and
prosperous
livelihood
model
among
various
ethnic
groups
in
Ji-
ulong
County
demonstrates
a
structural
coupling
and
dynamic
adjustment
mechanisms
of
multi-eth-
nic
intertwining
and
symbiosis,
distilling
the
local-
ized
wisdom
of
social
integration
in
multi-ethnic
ar-
eas.
The
transformation
of
livelihood
modes
is
closely
linked
to
the
development
of
ethnic
rela-
tions.
Livelihood
transitions
across
different
histori-
cal
periods
not
only
test
the
wisdom
and
strength
of
people
from
all
ethnic
groups
but
also
give
rise
to
innovative
possibilities
for
symbiotic
order.
Throughout
this
process,
institutional
safeguards
and
resource
allocation
mechanisms
furnish
struc-
tural
underpinnings
for
the
collaborative
develop-
ment
of
multiple
ethnic
groups,
enabling
a
gradual
shift
in
ethnic
relations
from
spatial
interdepend-
ence
to
cultural
amalgamation.
Such
a
develop-
ment
path
enables
all
ethnic
groups
in
Jiulong
County
to
exchange
and
learn
from
each
other,
en-
hance
their
sense
of
identity,
build
up
a
social
foundation
of
win-win
cooperation,
strengthen
the
consciousness
of
a
community
with
a
shared
future,
and
provide
inclusive
and
innovative
local
experi-
ence
for
the
deep
construction
of
Chinese
national
community.
