social and cultural nature of the minority peoples. So it is that if we are

of the speakers of the ancestral language subsequently spread into different

Besides what we have related above, there are many other ethnic
groups who are less known to us. They number altogether a million or so.
Most of them have been sinicized or are under process of acculturation
and even of physical assimilation, so that their original distinctions have
become blurred and, therefore, reduced in many instances to differences in
language and minor social usages (Mickey, 1947, Preface by Coon). It
seems somewhat likely that the trend is turning in the direction of further
intermingling of all the minorities and the elimination of all ethnic
differences, although no one can tell you how long or how far it will run.
